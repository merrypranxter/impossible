# Wrong Materials

Materials in this repo should imply laws, not just surfaces.

## Material families

### Soft Boolean Bone
Creamy, smooth, wax-like seams where architecture has grown into itself.

### Liminal Carpet
Repeating noisy plane material, low contrast, slightly hostile.

### Phosphor Concrete
Concrete lit like an old CRT.

### Inversion Glass
Reflective surface that samples a domain-inverted coordinate.

### Chlorine Marble
Pool-like material with watery caustic lies.

### Black Mouth Membrane
Dark portal skin with rim glow and inward-pulling normals.

### Symbolic Plaster
Matte wall surface interrupted by sigils generated from polar coordinates.

### Fossilized Fog
Surfaces appear carved from volumetric density.

## Material rule

Every material should answer:

> What impossible law has this surface been exposed to for too long?

Example:

A wall in a twisted stairwell should not just be gray.  
It should have stretched stripe logic, stress bands, and tangent-swirl shading because it has been twisted for geological emotional time.

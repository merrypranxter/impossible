# Suno / Music Prompt Ideas

These are style prompts for scoring impossible raymarched worlds.

## Style block: haunted shader atrium

Instrumental only, slow pulsing analog synth score for a first-person drift through impossible raymarched architecture; early-1980s experimental video installation mood, unstable CRT glow, modular synth drones, sub-bass gravity hum, glassy FM bells, distant choir pads, tape wow, reversed cymbal swells, granular fog texture, subtle clockwork percussion, ominous but beautiful, liminal backrooms atmosphere, psychedelic sci-fi cathedral, no rock drums, no lyrics.

## Style block: wrong physics motel

Dry, deadpan spoken-word optional, minimal synth funk undercurrent, cheap motel neon, VHS hiss, rubbery bass, slow drum machine, uneasy lounge chords, shimmering phaser, warped tape, distant elevator bell, procedural dread, absurd haunted math energy, not cute, not emo, not cinematic trailer bullshit.

## Style block: portal mouth rave

Instrumental acid-electro shader rave, rubber 303 bass, fast arpeggiated lasers, chopped breath samples, CRT scanline percussion, cyberdelic portal energy, aggressive but playful, neon wrong-gravity club inside a cursed GLSL fragment shader.
